The web app installs like a native app on desktop, phone, and tablet. It covers diary, progress charts, AI entry with your own key, and shared JSON export. The Android app adds Health Connect, widgets, notifications, and on-device Gemma 4.

What you get

Food logging that stays on your device unless you choose cloud AI, plus a few extras that make logging easier.

  • [*]
    Android and browser. Installable PWA and Android app share the same diary and body-metrics JSON. No cloud sync between clients.
  • [*]
    Bring your own AI key. Cloud provider of your choice, or on Android run Gemma 4 on-device. Analysis shows progress steps and fills fields as the model replies.
  • [*]
    Meal components. After AI entry, expand ingredients, edit grams per row, and keep meal totals in sync.
  • [*]
    Recipes and saved meals. Save recents, favorites, and multi-ingredient recipes for one-tap logging.
  • [*]
    Live calorie budget. The Android home ring grows your daily goal with the day's burn: measured by Health Connect, a manual entry after a run, or an estimate from your history.
  • [*]
    Barcode and Open Food Facts. Scan barcodes against Open Food Facts (4.6M+ products, cached offline), or let photo analysis read codes from the image.
  • [*]
    Diet modes and Health Connect. Keto and other diet modes on both clients. Android syncs steps, exercise, sleep, heart rate, and hydration, two-way with wearables and scales.
  • [*]
    Open data, published accuracy. FOSS barcode on Android, open export and import, and published AI accuracy notes instead of a single marketing number.

The numbers

No ads, no analytics SDKs, no accounts. Food logs and API keys stay on your device, encrypted at rest. Learn more in the privacy policy.
