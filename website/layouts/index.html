What you get

Food logging that stays on your device unless you choose cloud AI, plus extras that make daily use clearer.

  • Android and browser

    Installable PWA and Android app share the same diary and body-metrics JSON. No cloud sync between clients.

  • AI you control

    Bring your own cloud key. On Android, opt into on-device Gemma 4. Analysis shows progress steps and fills fields as the model replies.

  • Meal components

    After AI entry, expand ingredients, edit grams per row, and keep meal totals in sync.

  • Recipes and saved meals

    Save recents, favorites, and multi-ingredient recipes for one-tap logging.

  • Diet modes and Health Connect

    Keto and other diet modes on both clients. Android syncs steps, exercise, sleep, heart rate, and hydration through Health Connect.

  • Open data, honest accuracy

    FOSS barcode on Android, open export and import, and published AI accuracy notes instead of a single marketing number.

Built on open source

Chompass is based on Fud AI by Apoorv Darshan. Same BYOK food logging roots, plus an Android app and browser PWA on Codeberg. Prefer workouts and the upstream feature set? See Fud AI on GitHub.
