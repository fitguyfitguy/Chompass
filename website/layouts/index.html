Built on open source

Chompass is a privacy-focused fork of Fud AI by Apoorv Darshan. Same BYOK AI food logging, plus an Android app and browser PWA on Codeberg. If you want workouts and the upstream feature set, check out Fud AI on GitHub.

Privacy-focused food tracking

Forked from Fud AI to stay ad-free on Codeberg. Snap, speak, scan, share, or type. Food text and photos stay on your device unless you choose cloud AI.

Features

Multi-photo logging, keto modes, open export, FOSS barcode, and a browser PWA.

Install

Obtainium or APK, or the installable browser PWA.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics SDKs. API keys encrypted at rest. BYOK cloud or on-device inference (Android).
