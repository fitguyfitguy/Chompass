Initial public release of NoFUD - an ad-free, privacy-focused Android fork of Fud AI.

• No ads or analytics SDKs
• Original NoFUD branding and launcher icon
• AI food logging, Coach, workouts, Health Connect, widgets, diary export
• Meal sharing via nofud:// deep links; imports upstream fudai:// links
• Exercise photos converted to WebP (~19 MB bundle)
