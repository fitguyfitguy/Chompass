NoFUD is an ad-free, privacy-focused calorie tracker forked from Fud AI. Log meals with photos, voice, barcode scans, or text. Bring your own cloud AI key (Google AI Studio, OpenRouter, etc.) or use optional on-device Gemma 4 inference. Nothing is sent to a server unless you choose a cloud provider.

<b>Food logging</b>
• Multi-photo capture, share-into-app, voice, barcode, manual entry, saved meals and recipes
• Open Food Facts barcode lookup
• Optional on-device AI (Gemma 4 via LiteRT-LM): one-time model download, opt-in, arm64/x86_64 with 6 GB+ RAM

<b>Progress &amp; health</b>
• Weight, body fat, calorie history, goals, steps and exercise
• Health Connect sync (read/write meals, weight, body fat, height; read sleep, heart rate, hydration, energy)
• Optional local water tracking and home-screen widgets

<b>Privacy</b>
• No ads, no analytics SDKs, no accounts
• Local-first storage; encrypted API keys on device
• Diary and body-metrics export (JSON, Markdown, CSV)
